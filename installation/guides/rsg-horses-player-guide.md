## Getting Your First Horse

- Horses are bought at the **Valentine stable**. Open the stable menu and choose **Buy Horse**.
- Click a horse to **preview** it. Use the **arrow buttons** to rotate the preview.
- Click **BUY**, enter a name, pick Gelding or Mare.
- Sell price is always **half** of the buy price, so choose carefully.

## Stable Menu

Visit any stable and open the menu:

| Option      | What it does                                             |
|-------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| View Horses | See your horses, stats, and pick one to ride              |
| Sell Horse  | Sell a horse back for 50% of its buy price               |
| Move Horse  | Relocate a horse to another stable (small distance fee)  |
| Trade Horse | Give your active horse to a nearby player                |
| Breed Horse | Breed two of your horses (breeder job required)          |
| Horse Shop  | Buy brushes, feed, lanterns, holsters and more           |
| Store Horse | Put your current horse away at this stable               |
| Customize   | Change tack and colours (Valentine only)                 |

Only horses stored **at the stable you are standing at** are listed.

## Calling, Riding and Storing

- Set a horse **active** from View Horses, then call it the usual way. It will come to you if it is nearby and on a road is best.
- To put it away, walk to any stable and choose **Store Horse**.
- Your active horse follows you as a companion and has its own inventory (saddlebag required, bigger horses carry more as they level).

## Horse Levels and Bonding

- Horses earn **XP** from feeding, up to **level 10**. Higher level means more health, stamina, speed and inventory space.
- **Bonding** grows with XP too (4 levels). High bond unlocks tricks:
  - **Lay down** — from 1000 XP
  - **Play** — from 2000 XP
- Check a horse's level, XP and cleanliness in View Horses.

## Customization

At **Valentine**, open Customize with your horse out:

- **Components** — blankets, saddles, horns, saddlebags, stirrups, bedrolls, tails, manes, masks. Step through options with the **arrows** (0 = off). Each changed part has a small fee.
- **Coat & Markings** — main coat colour, white markings (set to **255 = none**), nose/muzzle shade, plus **mane and tail colours**. One flat fee covers all colour changes.
- Rotate the preview with the arrows to inspect from every side.
- **Save** charges you and stores everything; **Cancel** restores the old look.
- The price is shown live as **TOTAL** before you save.

## Horse Care

- **Brush** (horse brush item) — cleans dirt off. Dirty horses perform worse.
- **Feed** — carrots, apples, hay and sugar restore health/stamina. Stimulant fully restores.
- **Lantern** (horse lantern item) — equip a lantern on your horse for night travel.
- **Holster** (horse holster item) — equip a holster, then store a long-arm weapon (rifle, shotgun, repeater, bow) on the horse and retrieve it later.
- **Reviver** (horse reviver item) — revive your horse if it goes down. You have a short grace period to attempt a revive.
- Near water troughs and hay piles your horse can **drink** and **graze** on its own.

## Horse Shop

The stable Horse Shop stocks: brush, lantern, carrot, apple, hay, stimulant, reviver and holster. Add items to the cart and purchase with cash. Stock is limited per item.

## Trading, Moving and Selling

- **Trade**: stand next to another player with your active horse out, choose Trade, accept with `/accepttrade` on their side (requests expire after 30 seconds).
- **Move**: pick a destination stable; fee is a base price plus distance.
- **Sell**: instant, pays 50% of buy price, cannot be undone.

## Breeding

- Requires the **horsebreeder** job.
- Pick a **mare** (female, mature, not pregnant, off cooldown) then a **stallion** (male, mature).
- Costs **1x hay** per attempt.
- Gestation takes about **2 hours**, then the foal appears in the mare's stable.
- Foals start small and grow to full size over about **12 hours**. Foal names, gender and breed are random.
- Mares need a **2 hour** cooldown between breedings.

## Wild Horses

- Tame a wild horse by calming and mounting it in the wild.
- Ride it to a **frontier seller** (Van Horn, Valentine or Blackwater — marked with blips) and open the menu:
  - **SELL HORSE** — an appraisal bar runs (stay still; ESC cancels). On success you get cash based on breed plus a water item. A 15-minute cooldown applies between sales.
  - **SAVE HORSE** — register it: give it a name and gender and it joins your Valentine stable as a normal owned horse (adult, no XP).
- You cannot sell or save a horse you already own, or one someone else tamed — it must be **your** taming.
- Admins can flag a mount wild for events with `/sethorsewild`.

| Near horse     | Context prompts: brush, feed, lantern, holster, inventory, tricks |

## Death and Ageing

- If your horse is critically injured you will get a warning — use a **reviver fast**.
- A dead horse is gone for good (its inventory is lost with it).
- Horses age: starter horses live about a week, regular horses a very long time. You will get a telegram if one passes of old age.
